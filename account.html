What an account stores

Your email, a password Supabase stores only as a hash, and the walkthrough answers you choose to save: the same answers Save my answers to a file writes, kept in a database instead of your downloads.

Never your documents, and never a Social Security number, employer, address or school. The walkthrough has no field for any of them.

This is a student project

For the capstone demo, use practice figures rather than your real income. You can delete a plan or the whole account at any time from this page.
