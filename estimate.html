What this leaves out

Education credits, taxable scholarship money, investment income and losses, marketplace insurance reconciliation, and state tax. Those can move the number by hundreds of dollars in either direction.

Treat this as a ballpark, not a plan.

Two things it will show you

If you are claimed as a dependent, your standard deduction shrinks to your earned income plus $450, floored at $1,350. Most students never see this explained anywhere.

If you had gig income, self-employment tax appears as a separate line, along with the deduction for half of it. The tax is the number that surprises people, because nothing was withheld to cover it.
