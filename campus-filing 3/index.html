Campus Filing is a student project. It is educational software, not tax preparation, and it does not transmit returns. Figures reflect tax year 2026 rules published in IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-32 and should be confirmed against IRS Publication 501, Publication 970, and your state revenue department before you file.

No Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, or uploaded documents are collected or transmitted.
