Privacy and security

We ask about your situation, never your identity

Tax software has to know who you are. This tool does not, so it does not ask.

What we never collect

There is no field anywhere on this site for any of the following, and adding one would be a design failure rather than a feature request.

  • Social Security numbers or ITINs
  • Bank account or routing numbers
  • Dates of birth, addresses, or phone numbers
  • Employer names or your school's name
  • Anything that identifies you personally

What happens to your answers

The walkthrough runs entirely in your browser. Your answers live in page memory and are gone the moment you close the tab. Nothing is written to a server, a database, or browser storage.

That is also why there is no save button yet. Saving requires an account, an account requires a database, and a database holding student income figures is a responsibility this project takes seriously rather than casually.

What happens to uploaded documents

Files you drop into the walkthrough are read in the browser and never uploaded. They are not sent anywhere, not stored, and not retained after you leave the page. In the current build, document reading is simulated for demonstration and no real parsing occurs.

How to verify this yourself

Open your browser's developer tools, switch to the Network tab, and run the entire walkthrough. You will see requests for fonts and stylesheets and nothing else. No request carries your answers.

Why this matters more than usual

A tax tool is an attractive target precisely because of what people type into one. The safest way to protect information is not to hold it.

Planned account features

A future version will let you save a profile and keep each year's documents in one place. When that is built, this page will state exactly what is stored, where, and for how long, before the feature ships rather than after.

The demonstration version of that feature will use generated sample profiles, not real student data.

Reporting a problem

If you find something on this site that handles information in a way this page does not describe, tell the project team. That is a bug, not an edge case.
