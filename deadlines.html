Tax year 2026

Every date that matters

The return you are filing covers calendar year 2026 and is due April 15, 2027. Everything below is on the way there.

The season, in order

  • Now through December
    Get your paperwork straightConfirm your employer has your current address, since W-2s go to the address on file. If you did gig work, total up your mileage and expenses now rather than reconstructing them in April.
  • Mid-January
    IRS Free File opensFree File partners begin accepting returns before the IRS starts processing them, holding your return until the season opens. Note that IRS Direct File no longer exists. The IRS told its 25 partner states in November 2025 that it would not run in the 2026 season and set no future launch date.
  • January 31
    W-2 and 1099 deadlineEmployers and gig platforms must issue these by this date. Most post them to a payroll portal like Workday or ADP rather than mailing anything.
  • Late January
    IRS begins processingE-filed returns start moving. Filing in the first two weeks generally means the fastest refund.
  • January 31
    1098-T and 1098-E arriveYour school posts the 1098-T to your student account portal. Loan servicers issue the 1098-E if you paid $600 or more in interest.
  • Mid-February
    1099-B for investmentsBrokerages have a later deadline than employers. If you sold stock or crypto, do not file before this arrives or you will be amending.
  • February onward
    Campus tax clinics runVITA sites open on most campuses and fill up fast. Book early and bring your document checklist.
  • April 15, 2027
    Federal and most state returns dueAlso the deadline to request an extension. An extension gives you more time to file, not more time to pay.
  • October 15
    Extended returns dueIf you filed for an extension, this is the final date. Interest has been accruing on anything owed since April.

Deadlines that catch students out

Refunds expire after three years

If you never filed for an earlier year and were owed money, you have three years from that year's original deadline to claim it. After that the money belongs to the Treasury permanently. This is the most common way students lose real dollars.

An extension is not a payment extension

Filing Form 4868 moves your filing deadline to October. It does not move the payment deadline. Anything owed still accrues interest from April.

Quarterly payments if you freelance

If gig income continues year to year, you may owe estimated payments on Form 1040-ES, due in April, June, September, and the following January. Missing them adds a penalty even if you pay in full at filing.

File on time even if you cannot pay

The failure to file penalty is roughly ten times the failure to pay penalty. Filing without payment and setting up an IRS installment plan costs far less than staying silent.

Still owed money from a past year?

You can file 2023, 2024, and 2025 returns now. Each needs that year's forms and that year's software.
